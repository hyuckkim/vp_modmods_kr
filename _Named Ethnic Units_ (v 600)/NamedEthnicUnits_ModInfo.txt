[h2]Adds [b]Ethnic Units[/b] with [b]Unique Names[/b] for [b]EACH[/b] Civilization. Standalone![/h2]
[quote]Named Ethnic Units enhances Civilization V with over 250 unique units, each specifically named to reflect the historical and cultural identity of its civilization. Spanning all 43 civilizations, this mod brings depth and authenticity to your armies, offering a distinct identity for every unit on the battlefield.

Each unit has been carefully designed and rigorously tested to ensure stable, crash-free gameplay. The standard unit scale from the base game is preserved, ensuring compatibility and visual consistency with other mods.

From the M4A3E8 Sherman for America to the Iqhawe for the Zulu, every unit carries a unique name, enriching the cultural immersion and adding a personal touch to your campaigns. For a full list of units, see the Named Ethnic Unit List in the pinned discussions.
[/quote]

[h3]Compatibility[/h3]
For best results, disable other unit model mods to avoid conflicts.

[h3]Known Issues[/h3]
Reloading a game causes unique names to revert to default names, unless you are using the Core Mod, in which case names are preserved. Unique graphics remain intact either way.

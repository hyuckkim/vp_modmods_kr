v1 (changes from JFD's release)
--------------------------------------------------------------
UA: Apostolic See
- Removed +1 Faith, -1 Culture to Palace (0 Culture in Palace is extremely crippling, especially in VP).
- Added +3 Faith to Palace, Great Works, National Wonders, and World Wonders. (does not include Tradition buildings)
- Removed can purchase Disciples (Disciples are now the UU, mainly so that it works better with the AI).
- Added all Missionaries get +50% religious strength.

UB: Chapel (replaces Temple)
- Increased number of Great Work slots to 2 from 1.
- Bonus from filling the Great Work slot now coded as a theming bonus when filled with your Great Works (AI understands that better, and it looks nicer in the UI).
- Theming yield is 3 (culture and tourism), and the bonus is 3 faith.
- Costs less Production, 100 instead of 200.
- No Gold maintenance.
- All three Chapels have the same Religious Pressure and Unhappiness modifiers as the Temple.
- First Chapel now has Music slots (to be the same as the Temple).
- Second Chapel now has Art or Artifact slots (they are scarce early game, with only Palace and the Tradition building available until Medieval).
- Third Chapel now has Writing slots (Amphitheaters unlock fairly early).
- First Chapel gets the same resource yield bonuses as Temple.
- Second and Third Chapel no longer shows up in the leader screen, and no longer requires a certain amount of followers of your religion.

UU: Disciple (standalone)
- Now a UU rather than part of the UA.
- Can only be Faith bought in the capital, and has 8 turns of purchase cooldown (on Standard).
- Has a base Faith cost of 300.
- Faith cost scales in the same way as other regular Faith purchases (Missionaries, Inquisitors, etc).

UU: Swiss Guard (replaces Landsknecht with More UCs)
- Now a City State gift only unit (unless More UCs is installed).
- Can only be purchased with Gold or Faith.
- Does not need the Authority finisher for Gold purchase.
- Does not need Zealotry for Faith purchase.
- Tourism bonus changed to a 10% yield modifier when garrisoned (retained on upgrade).
- Capital defense heal now changed to 25HP heal on all kills (retained on upgrade).

UW: Vatican Museums (replaces Hermitage with More UCs)
- 1 additional Great Work of Art slot.
- Free Great Artist.
- +25% Culture in the city instead of +10%.
- 1 Culture per 2 Citizens instead of per 4.
- +10 Gold from when themed instead of +5.
- +10 bonus Culture and Tourism when themed, instead of +6.
- 25% of Culture from World Wonders, Natural Wonders, Improvements added to Tourism, 25% more Tourism from Great Works. Affects all cities. Same effect as National Visitor Centre (stacks).
- +2 Tourism to Chapels (all three types).

Event: The Holy See (for Enginseer's Community Event)
- Choice 1: Free Great Person.
- Choice 2: -25% Culture cost of acquiring new tiles, +1 Gold from Farms, Camps, and Pastures.
- Choice 3: +2 Faith, Culture, and Tourism to all Great Works.
- Choice 4: All units gain Faith and Culture when killing an enemy unit (equal to 50% of the enemy's combat strength).
- Choice 5: -25% reduction of religious unrest and poverty.
- Choice 6: +4 Faith and Culture, and +1 Tourism in holy city or capital for every 10 followers of your religion (yours and foreign cities).

Event: Banquet of Chestnuts
- Yield values now scale with era.
- Choice 1: Golden Age Points gained increased to 600.
- Choice 2: Faith gained increased to 300.

Event: Inquisition
- Yield values now scale with era.
- Choice 2: Now requires 60 Gold, and grants 30 Faith.

Decision: Declare the Kingdom of God
- Costs decreased, but now scale with era (about the same in Renaissance).
- Now requires 1 Magistrate.
- Removed 2 Magistrate reward.
- New reward: spawns 1 free unit in your capital per 5 cities following your religion whenever a war begins.

Decision: Institute the Societas Jesu
- Costs decreased, but now scale with era (about the same in Renaissance).
- Now has a gold cost as well (same cost as faith).
- Changed effect from 50% of your population converted per turn to 15% less faith cost for missionaries, inquisitors, and buildings (previous one was far too strong).

Compatibility: JFD's Cultural Diversity
- Will now correctly use the Southern Papal soundtracks.
